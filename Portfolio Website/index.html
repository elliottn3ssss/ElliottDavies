Elliott Davies | Stage Technician & LED Video Wall Specialist

Built to Scale.
Running on Cue.

Independent stage technician and LED video wall specialist based in Northwest Arkansas, supporting arena tours, Broadway theatricals, and regional live events. Working contract stagecraft for Ozark Production Services alongside deck crew calls for the Walton Arts Center and Walmart AMP, I deliver fast, disciplined physical execution on high-pressure, time-critical load-ins.

My strength is rapid hardware adaptation on deck: whether flying a ground-supported 60-panel wall solo under an hour or building 240+ panel walls hung from truss, I read and assemble modular locking systems, fly bars, and frame hardware on sight. Operating strictly on deck and handling ground/down-rigging support, I focus on the details that prevent show-stops—dead-level pick points, strain relief, diode and module protection, balanced multi-circuit distro power, and redundant signal topology.

Technical directors get a technician who shows up fully PPE-compliant with their own pack, reads the hardware without needing hand-holding, communicates clearly, and stays on deck until every run is loomed, every panel passes test, and the trucks are packed.

  • Down-Rigging & Header Rig-Bar Attachment
